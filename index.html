          About

          What is this?

          Smart Expense Tracker is a website that helps you track daily expenses and manage your budget. It shows your balance, spending by category and your progress towards a savings goal.

          Built with

          HTML for structure, CSS for the notebook look and night mode, and JavaScript for all the logic. The chart is drawn with the Canvas API, with no libraries.

          Where is my data?

          In your browser's Local Storage, on this device only. Clearing your browser data removes it, so use Export CSV on the Transactions page to keep a copy.

          Tips

          Use the quick-add buttons for daily small spends, set a monthly budget first, and check Insights at the end of each week.

          Features

          Income & expenses, categories, budget bar, savings goal, edit & undo, search, month picker, CSV export, smart notes and night mode.

          Good to know

          Numbers are in Indian rupees (₹). Your Remaining balance is all-time, while income, spending and the chart follow the month you select.
